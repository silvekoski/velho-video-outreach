# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Velho's growth team uses Signal Room to prepare a first conversation with an established company. Typical buyers are CEOs, CFOs, COOs and operations leaders. They are busy and receive generic AI pitches every day.

## Product Purpose

Turn a timely public signal into a thoughtful, company-specific conversation about the manual work that may be slowing growth. Help Velho get into a room with the right buyer by offering a concrete first proof, not a broad AI pitch.

## Brand Personality

Observant, clear-eyed and useful. Signal Room feels like a field notebook for a sharp operator: warm paper, forest ink, succinct evidence and room for a human point of view.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI copy or unsupported claims about a company
- Fake prospect research presented as fact
- Auto-sent outreach, scraped personal data or hidden tracking
- Template dashboards built from interchangeable metric cards

## Design Principles

- Put the observed signal and its source before the interpretation.
- Label every operational claim as a hypothesis to validate.
- Make the first proof small enough to discuss in a 20-minute working session.
- Keep company input, evidence, discovery question and message draft in one reviewable flow.
- Keep outbound drafts unsent and visible as drafts.
- Use fictional records only as clearly marked interaction examples.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, native form controls, keyboard-visible focus, clear validation and a responsive layout. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep contrast strong and avoid relying on color alone to convey signal state.
